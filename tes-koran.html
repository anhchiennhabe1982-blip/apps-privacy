Ringkasan

Tes Koran membantu Anda berlatih tes Kraepelin dan Pauli untuk psikotes. Aplikasi ini tidak memakai akun dan tidak punya server sendiri. Data latihan tersimpan di ponsel. Versi gratis menampilkan iklan, dan penyedia iklan dapat mengumpulkan data perangkat seperti dijelaskan di bawah.

Penilaian di aplikasi hanya panduan latihan dan bukan hasil psikotes resmi.

Iklan

Aplikasi gratis ini menampilkan iklan. Tergantung dari toko tempat Anda memasang aplikasi, iklan disediakan oleh:

  • Google AdMob pada versi Google Play dan Xiaomi GetApps. Kebijakan Privasi Google
  • Huawei Petal Ads pada versi Huawei AppGallery. Pernyataan Privasi Huawei

SDK iklan tersebut dapat mengumpulkan ID iklan perangkat, alamat IP, informasi perangkat dan aplikasi, serta interaksi Anda dengan iklan. Data itu dipakai untuk menampilkan dan mengukur iklan serta mencegah penipuan. Pengembang tidak menerima informasi pribadi Anda dari mereka. Anda dapat mengatur ulang ID iklan atau mematikan personalisasi iklan di pengaturan ponsel.

Anda dapat menonton video berhadiah (opsional) untuk membuka tes versi penuh.

Summary

Tes Koran helps you practise the Kraepelin and Pauli tests used in psychological assessments. It has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your practice data stays on your phone. The free version shows ads, and the ad provider may collect device data as described below.

The scores are a practice guide only and are not an official psychological assessment.

Who we are

Tes Koran (package com.tiennguyen.teskoran) is published by an independent developer, Tien Nguyen, on Huawei AppGallery, Xiaomi GetApps and Google Play.

Information the app uses

DataWhyWhere it goes
Practice historyAnswers and mistakes per column, practice dates and test settings, used to show your charts and history.Stored on your phone only. Never uploaded.

The app does not ask for location, camera, contacts or storage permissions. Internet access is used only to load ads.

Advertising

The free app shows ads. Depending on where you installed it, ads are served by:

  • Google AdMob in the Google Play and Xiaomi GetApps versions. Google Privacy Policy
  • Huawei Petal Ads in the Huawei AppGallery version. Huawei Privacy Statement

These ad SDKs may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. They use it to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. The developer does not receive your personal information from them. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's settings.

You can watch an optional rewarded video to unlock a full-length test.

Keeping and deleting your data

Use How it works → Delete history, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • We do not run analytics or tracking of our own.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
