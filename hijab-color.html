Hijab Color Match – Privacy Policy

Android app · Effective 10 October 2026

EnglishBahasa Indonesia

Summary

Hijab Color Match finds your seasonal color palette from a selfie and suggests hijab colors that suit you. Your photo is analyzed on your phone and is never uploaded. The app has no accounts and no servers of its own. The free version shows ads, and the ad provider may collect device data as described below.

Who we are

Hijab Color Match (package com.tiennguyen.hijabcolor) is published by an independent developer, Tien Nguyen, on Huawei AppGallery, Xiaomi GetApps and Google Play.

Information the app uses

DataWhyWhere it goes
Your selfieFind your face and read the color of your cheeks to work out your skin tone and season.Processed on your phone only. A small cropped copy is kept inside the app so it can show your result. It is never uploaded.
Scarf photosRead the color of a scarf you photograph and say whether it suits you.Processed on your phone and not stored.

Face detection uses the face detector built into Android. It finds where your face is in the picture and does not identify who you are. No face data or biometric template is created or shared.

The app does not ask for the camera or storage permission: it opens your phone's camera app or photo picker, and you choose which picture to give it.

Advertising

The free app shows ads. Depending on where you installed it, ads are served by:

  • Google AdMob in the Google Play and Xiaomi GetApps versions. Google Privacy Policy
  • Huawei Petal Ads in the Huawei AppGallery version. Huawei Privacy Statement

These ad SDKs may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. They use it to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. The developer does not receive your personal information from them. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's settings.

You can watch an optional rewarded video to unlock the full palette for 24 hours.

Keeping and deleting your data

Everything the app stores lives on your phone. Use Settings → Delete my photo and results, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app to delete it.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • We do not run analytics or tracking of our own.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Ringkasan

Warna Hijab Cocok menemukan palet warna musiman Anda dari foto selfie dan menyarankan warna hijab yang cocok. Foto Anda dianalisis di ponsel dan tidak pernah diunggah. Aplikasi ini tidak memakai akun dan tidak punya server sendiri. Versi gratis menampilkan iklan, dan penyedia iklan dapat mengumpulkan data perangkat seperti dijelaskan di bawah.

Siapa kami

Warna Hijab Cocok (paket com.tiennguyen.hijabcolor) diterbitkan oleh pengembang independen, Tien Nguyen, di Huawei AppGallery, Xiaomi GetApps, dan Google Play.

Aplikasi tidak meminta izin kamera atau penyimpanan: aplikasi membuka aplikasi kamera atau pemilih foto di ponsel, dan Anda sendiri yang memilih gambarnya.

Iklan

Aplikasi gratis ini menampilkan iklan. Tergantung dari toko tempat Anda memasang aplikasi, iklan disediakan oleh:

  • Google AdMob pada versi Google Play dan Xiaomi GetApps. Kebijakan Privasi Google
  • Huawei Petal Ads pada versi Huawei AppGallery. Pernyataan Privasi Huawei

SDK iklan tersebut dapat mengumpulkan ID iklan perangkat, alamat IP, informasi perangkat dan aplikasi, serta interaksi Anda dengan iklan. Data itu dipakai untuk menampilkan dan mengukur iklan serta mencegah penipuan. Pengembang tidak menerima informasi pribadi Anda dari mereka. Anda dapat mengatur ulang ID iklan atau mematikan personalisasi iklan di pengaturan ponsel.

Anda dapat menonton video berhadiah (opsional) untuk membuka palet lengkap selama 24 jam.

Penyimpanan dan penghapusan data

Semua yang disimpan aplikasi ada di ponsel Anda. Gunakan Pengaturan → Hapus foto dan hasilku, hapus data aplikasi, atau copot aplikasi untuk menghapusnya.

Yang tidak kami lakukan

  • Kami tidak meminta Anda membuat akun.
  • Kami tidak menjalankan analitik atau pelacakan sendiri.
  • Kami tidak menjual atau membagikan informasi pribadi Anda.

Anak-anak

Aplikasi ini tidak ditujukan untuk anak di bawah 13 tahun dan tidak dengan sengaja mengumpulkan informasi pribadi anak.
