Salah Times – Privacy Policy

Prayer Times & Qibla app for Android · Effective 5 October 2026

Englishالعربية

Summary

Salah Times shows prayer times and the Qibla direction, and includes the Qur’an, daily azkar, a tasbih counter, a prayer tracker, an Islamic calendar and a Ramadan mode. It has no user accounts and no servers of its own. Your location, settings and everything you log stay on your phone. The free version shows ads, and the ad provider may collect device data as described below.

Who we are

Salah Times (package com.tiennguyen.salahtimes) is published by an independent developer, Tien Nguyen, on Huawei AppGallery, Xiaomi GetApps and Google Play. You can reach the developer by email at trungtien1571992@gmail.com.

Advertising

The free app shows banner ads. Depending on where you installed it, ads are served by:

  • Google AdMob in the Google Play and Xiaomi GetApps versions. Google Privacy Policy
  • Huawei Petal Ads in the Huawei AppGallery version. Huawei Privacy Statement

These ad SDKs may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. They use it to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. The developer does not receive your personal information from them. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in your phone’s settings.

Permissions

  • Location: calculate prayer times for your position.
  • Notifications: alert you when each prayer time begins.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms): deliver those alerts on time.
  • Run at startup: re-schedule reminders after the phone restarts or the time zone changes.
  • Internet: load ads.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • We do not run analytics or tracking of our own.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.

Keeping and deleting your data

Everything the app stores lives on your phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your phone’s settings, deletes it.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
