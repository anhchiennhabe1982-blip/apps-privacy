Ringkasan

Latihan Ujian SIM membantu Anda berlatih soal teori ujian SIM A dan SIM C. Aplikasi ini tidak memakai akun dan tidak punya server sendiri. Progres belajar Anda tersimpan di ponsel. Versi gratis menampilkan iklan, dan penyedia iklan dapat mengumpulkan data perangkat seperti dijelaskan di bawah.

Ini bukan aplikasi resmi Korlantas Polri dan tidak berafiliasi dengan instansi pemerintah mana pun.

Siapa kami

Latihan Ujian SIM (paket com.tiennguyen.ujiansim) diterbitkan oleh pengembang independen, Tien Nguyen, di Huawei AppGallery, Xiaomi GetApps, dan Google Play.

Aplikasi tidak meminta izin lokasi, kamera, kontak, atau penyimpanan. Izin internet hanya dipakai untuk memuat iklan.

Iklan

Aplikasi gratis ini menampilkan iklan. Tergantung dari toko tempat Anda memasang aplikasi, iklan disediakan oleh:

  • Google AdMob pada versi Google Play dan Xiaomi GetApps. Kebijakan Privasi Google
  • Huawei Petal Ads pada versi Huawei AppGallery. Pernyataan Privasi Huawei

SDK iklan tersebut dapat mengumpulkan ID iklan perangkat, alamat IP, informasi perangkat dan aplikasi, serta interaksi Anda dengan iklan. Data itu dipakai untuk menampilkan dan mengukur iklan serta mencegah penipuan. Pengembang tidak menerima informasi pribadi Anda dari mereka. Anda dapat mengatur ulang ID iklan atau mematikan personalisasi iklan di pengaturan ponsel.

Anda dapat menonton video berhadiah (opsional) untuk membuka simulasi ujian tambahan.

Penyimpanan dan penghapusan data

  • Kami tidak menjual atau membagikan informasi pribadi Anda.

Anak-anak

Summary

Latihan Ujian SIM helps you practise the theory test for Indonesian driving licences (SIM A and SIM C). It has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your study progress stays on your phone. The free version shows ads, and the ad provider may collect device data as described below.

This is not an official Korlantas Polri app and is not affiliated with any government agency.

Who we are

Latihan Ujian SIM (package com.tiennguyen.ujiansim) is published by an independent developer, Tien Nguyen, on Huawei AppGallery, Xiaomi GetApps and Google Play.

Information the app uses

DataWhyWhere it goes
Study progressQuestions answered right or wrong, mock exam scores, chosen licence type and your daily streak.Stored on your phone only. Never uploaded.

The app does not ask for location, camera, contacts or storage permissions. Internet access is used only to load ads.

Advertising

The free app shows ads. Depending on where you installed it, ads are served by:

  • Google AdMob in the Google Play and Xiaomi GetApps versions. Google Privacy Policy
  • Huawei Petal Ads in the Huawei AppGallery version. Huawei Privacy Statement

These ad SDKs may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. They use it to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. The developer does not receive your personal information from them. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's settings.

You can watch an optional rewarded video to unlock an extra mock exam.

Keeping and deleting your data

Use About → Delete all progress, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app to delete everything.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • We do not run analytics or tracking of our own.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
